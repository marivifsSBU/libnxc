# Authors

## libnxc 0.x (2020–2021), github.com/semodi/libnxc
- Sebastian Dick: design and original implementation (C++/Fortran library, pylibnxc, shipped
  models, documentation).
- Marivi Fernández-Serra (Stony Brook University): principal investigator.

## libnxc 2.0 (2026)
- Marivi Fernández-Serra (Stony Brook University): revival and rewrite. This covers the
  JAX/Equinox exporter, meta-GGA support, the CMake build, the Fortran module, the PySCF ≥ 2
  port, the Quantum ESPRESSO interface, and the tests and validation.

## Models
- Neural PBE clones (`GGA_XC_PBECLONE_*`): Sara Navarro-Rodríguez, Alec Wills, Kim J. Daas,
  María Camarasa-Gómez and Marivi Fernández-Serra (arXiv:2605.10331).
- `MGGA_XC_XCDIFF`, NN-PBE and NN-SCAN: Sebastian Dick.
- `*_HM`: Ryo Nagai, Ryosuke Akashi and Osamu Sugino, npj Comput. Mater. 6, 43 (2020).

The git history records the individual contributions.
